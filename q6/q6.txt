PC1 Regression Pattern:
The SST pattern shows the characteristic ENSO signal with warming/cooling
in the tropical Pacific. The wind vectors reveal the associated changes in
atmospheric circulation, with weakened trade winds during El Niño phases.

PC2 Regression Pattern:
